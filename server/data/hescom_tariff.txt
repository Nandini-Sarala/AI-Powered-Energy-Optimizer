HESCOM Tariff 2024 (Effective from 01.04.2023)

TARIFF SCHEDULE LT-1
Applicable to: installations serviced under Bhagya Jyothi and Kutira Jyothi (BJ/KJ) schemes.
Fixed Charges & Energy Charges:
- Commission Determined Tariff (CTD): 869 paise per unit subject to a monthly minimum of Rs.100 per installation per month.
- [CDT comprises of fixed charge component of Rs.4.88 and variable charge component of Rs.3.81 per unit]
NOTE:
(i) GOK is meeting the full cost of supply to BJ / KJ installations. However, if the GOK does not release the subsidy in advance, a Tariff of Rs.8.69 per unit subject to a monthly minimum of Rs.100 per installation per month, shall be demanded and collected from these consumers by HESCOM.
(ii) If the consumption exceeds 40 units per month or any BJ/KJ installation is found to have more than one out let, it shall be billed as per Tariff Schedule LT 2(a).

TARIFF SCHEDULE LT-2(a)
Applicable to: lighting/combined lighting, heating and motive Power installations of residential houses...
Fixed Charges / KW / Month:
- Per KW Upto 50 KW: Rs. 110/-
- For every additional KW above 50 KW: Rs. 210/-

Energy Charges per kWh (Unit):
- 0 to 100 Units: 475 paise (Rs. 4.75) per unit
- 0 to All Units [if the total consumption exceeds 100 units]: 700 paise (Rs. 7.00) per unit

Note:
Consequent to the merger of rural category under urban category, a rebate of 30 paise per unit shall be allowed to the consumers falling under village panchayat areas.

GENERAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS:
- 1 HP = 0.746 KW. 1HP = 0.878 KVA.
- The bill amount will be rounded off to the nearest Rupee.
- Belated payment: simple interest at 1% per month will be levied on the actual No. of days of delay subject to a minimum of Re.1 for LT installation.
- Incentive for Prompt Payment: 0.25% of the bill will be given if bill exceeds Rs. 1,00,000 and payment made 10 days in advance. Advance payment exceeding Rs. 1000 also earns incentive.
