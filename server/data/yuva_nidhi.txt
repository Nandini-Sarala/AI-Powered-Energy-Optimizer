Karnataka Yuva Nidhi Scheme

Highlights:
- Monthly Unemployment Allowance of Rs. 3,000/- per Month to Unemployed Graduated Youth.
- Monthly Unemployment Allowance of Rs. 1,500/- per Month to Unemployed Diploma Holder Youth.

Eligibility:
- Applicant should be a Permanent Resident of Karnataka.
- Applicant should be a Graduate or Diploma Holder.
- Applicant should passed his/ her Graduation or Diploma in the year 2022-2023.
- Applicant should be Unemployed for minimum 180 Days after the date of passing Graduation or Diploma.

Ineligibility:
- Those Youth who continue his/ her higher education after passing graduation or diploma.
- Beneficiary of Apprentice Wages.
- Youth Employed in Government or Private Sector.
- Self Employed Youth.

How to Apply:
- Through Karnataka Yuva Nidhi Online Application Form on Seva Sindhu Portal.
- Official Link: https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in
