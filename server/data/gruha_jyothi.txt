Gruha Jyothi Scheme Details

Scheme eligibility - Inclusion criteria:
1. Domestic installations only

Scheme eligibility - Exclusion criteria:
1. Commercial/industrial installations

Benefits to eligible households:
- Free, fixed monthly allowance of 'entitled units' per month, per domestic installation.
- Fixed monthly allowance equals average monthly consumption per consumer for FY 2022-23 plus additional units of 10% of average consumption, subject to maximum 200 units per month.
- New installations are entitled to 40 units per month.
- Amrut Jyothi beneficiaries (SC, ST, and BPL families) are entitled to 75 units + 10% per month.
- Bhagya Jyothi and Kuteera Jyothi installations are entitled to 53 units + 10% per month.
- Additional units above the entitled units have to be paid for, by each installation.
- However, if the monthly consumption is above 200 units, the beneficiary has to pay the bill fully for that month. The scheme benefit resumes from the following month.

Mode of delivery: reduction in electricity bill for the household.

Important Notes:
- With effect from 1 Feb 2024, domestic consumers using less than 48 units per month in the LT-2 category under the Gruhajyothi scheme, will be entitled to additional 10 units instead of 10% of average monthly consumption.
- Registration is mandatory on the Seva Sindhu portal and linking Aadhaar card is required.
